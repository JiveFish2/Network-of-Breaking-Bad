    How to read it. Each dot is a character; it grows with the number of scenes they have spoken in. The main characters wear their colour from the show's wardrobe palette (Walt white, then Blue Sky blue as Heisenberg; the dangerous ones darkened); everyone else takes their Louvain community's colour. Lines join characters who speak in the same scene (thicker = more scenes). The "Walter → Heisenberg" chart groups his scene partners by world: family, law, crime, other. ✝ marks the dead. A red dashed line from Walt means he is responsible: direct (he killed them), ordered (he had them killed) or indirect (they died as a consequence of his choices). Use ← → or space to move through the episodes.
